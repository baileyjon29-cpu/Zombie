LAST HAVEN

Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

In short: Last Haven does not collect, store or share any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Information we collect

None. Last Haven has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it does not use your device's advertising identifier.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Your current settlement (buildings, survivors, resources and day count)
  • Your Caps balance, purchased items and completed goals
  • Your best score and your sound, music and haptics settings

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. If your device backup includes app data (for example, iCloud Backup), Apple stores that backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details, Apple ID or billing information. Apple tells the app only which item was bought, so it can deliver it. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. You can restore non-consumable purchases at any time from the in-game menu.

Third parties

Last Haven contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. Apart from Apple's purchase system, the game does not connect to any outside service and is fully playable offline.

Children's privacy

Because Last Haven collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. The game is rated for players aged 9 and up and contains cartoon zombie violence.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled (for example, by adding online features), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released, and update the App Store privacy details to match.
